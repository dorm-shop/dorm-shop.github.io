## When and where

**May 11–13, 2027**  
American Society for Microbiology (ASM) headquarters  
1752 N St. NW  
Washington, DC 20036

The workshop is NSF-sponsored and convenes researchers studying dormancy across diverse organisms, disciplines, and biological scales.
