## Workshop goals

The workshop will bring researchers together to:

1. Develop shared terminology for dormancy traits and processes.
2. Review existing models and identify where new models are needed to understand dormancy life histories.
3. Identify conceptual and technical priorities for research across biological scales.
4. Explore how dormancy research can address societal challenges, including applications in agriculture and medicine.

The detailed agenda and participant presentations will be added when confirmed.

## Expected outcomes

The workshop aims to develop research priorities and collaborative writing projects, including publicly accessible white papers. Materials will be posted here after the meeting.
