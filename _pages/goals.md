## Workshop goals

The workshop will bring researchers together to:

1. Develop shared terminology for dormancy traits and processes across organisms and disciplines.
2. Connect existing models and identify where new models are needed to understand how dormancy is regulated and how it shapes living systems.
3. Identify conceptual and technical priorities for dormancy research across biological scales, while exploring how its principles might inform engineering, finance, and social systems.
4. Explore how dormancy research can address societal challenges, including applications in biotechnology, agriculture, and medicine.


## Expected outcomes

Participants will identify research priorities and launch collaborative writing projects, including publicly accessible white papers. These products will bring together insights across organisms and scales, clarify what we know about the regulation and consequences of dormancy, and set an agenda for future research and technological development.
